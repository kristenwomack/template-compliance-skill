# Internal template requirements

This is a fictional team's document used only as evaluation input. Its headings
are document sections, not a required identifier convention for customers.

## Ownership

Every template must name its owning team in its README.

## Support

Every template must document a support contact or support channel in its README.

## Production review

Templates intended for production must have a review by their owning team before
internal catalog publication. Development-only templates do not need this review.

## Recommendations

Consider adding a screenshot. This is optional, not a publication requirement.
